About

Peter (Ying Huang) is a seasoned Health Informatics executive with hands-on experience across the healthcare ecosystem, spanning care delivery, healthcare security, and patient-facing digital health services. His work spans the development and implementation of HIS, EHR, LIS, and PACS; pioneering contributions to the national-level policy framework, system architecture, and supporting information platforms underpinning China’s healthcare security system, with implementations across 30+ provincial-level regions and 300+ prefecture-level cities; and pioneering work in China’s online-to-offline healthcare services and sustainable digital health business models. At MedlinkAI, he leads the development and real-world application of healthcare AI agents for health search, care navigation, and intelligent service workflows.
